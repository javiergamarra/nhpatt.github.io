* **New house**, again.
* **Biking**: the 2016 numbers remain untouched. Picking up swimming instead, we'll see how that goes.

## Community

I also used to run **Liferay Agora**, the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/liferayagora) for all the meetups hosted at Liferay.
