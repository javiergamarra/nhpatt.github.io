So... it's been a while. My [last post](/new-year-2020/) was a set of 2020 resolutions written on January 1st, 2020, which is a genuinely funny thing to reread now. No promises this time about WIP limits or reading 52 books a year, just an update.

## Life

The big one: we had a kid. And then, apparently once wasn't enough, another. We're also buying a new house again.

The 2016 biking numbers remain untouched. Picking up swimming instead, we'll see how that goes. Reading dropped to almost nothing, but in its place I went back to gaming after 15 years.

## Work

I'm no longer at Liferay. I'm now a Staff Software Engineer at [Unity](https://unity.com), based in Valladolid.

## Community

Still around the usual suspects: [LechazoConf](https://lechazoconf.com), [WeCode](https://wecodefest.com) and [CyLicon Valley](https://cyliconvalley.es). I stepped down from the board of [Agile Spain](https://agile-spain.org) a while back.

Somewhere along the way I gave more than one hundred talks. I don't have an exact count anymore, but Codemotion, Commit Conf, T3chFest and CyLicon Valley all happened more than once. I also used to run **Liferay Agora**, the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/liferayagora) for all the meetups hosted at Liferay.

Six years is a long gap. Let's see if the next post takes less than six more.
